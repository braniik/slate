1.2 brings swipe-up search, system wallpaper, and group drag.

- Swipe up to search (turn on in settings): type part of an app's name and it opens once only one app matches.
- System wallpaper mode: Slate shows the wallpaper Android already has.
- Group drag: drag a multi-selection and it moves as one.
- Settings is now an opaque card.
- The add-apps picker matches fuzzily too.
